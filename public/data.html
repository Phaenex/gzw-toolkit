Your data

Everything you set on this site is kept in this browser's local storage, under the key . There are no accounts, no cookies, and nothing you enter is sent to a server. Clearing your browser's site data deletes it, so export a copy if it matters.

04 Where the numbers come from

  • Gear stats, vendor ranks, sizes, weights and pictures: each item's own page on the Gray Zone Warfare Fandom wiki. Every item links to its page.
  • Sell prices and the best vendor for each kind: the wiki's Loot page and item infoboxes, plus the Building Material and Disassembly Material categories.
  • The 54 kg limit: the wiki's Health system page.
  • Safes, landing zones, outposts and 0.5 site pins: the GZW Tac Map.
  • Hideout costs, runs and codes: the community guides credited on each line of the hideout page.
  • Buy prices are not published anywhere we could read, so the loadout builder asks you for them.
